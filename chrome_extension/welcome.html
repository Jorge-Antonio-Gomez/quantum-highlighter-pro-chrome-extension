How Quantum Highlighter PRO handles your data

Plain and short, before you make your first highlight:

  • The text you highlight, a few words around it, the address and title of the page, and the notes you write.
  • All of it stays in your browser, where Chrome may sync it with your Google account. Google Drive sync is optional and only starts if you connect it yourself.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer. There are no analytics, no ads, and your data is never sold or shared.
  • You can edit, export or delete everything at any time from the sidebar and the All annotations panel.
